NLOT · National Large Optical-Infrared Telescope

India's Big Eye to the Universe

NLOT is India's next-generation flagship ground-based telescope, planned for the Indian Astronomical Observatory at Hanle, Ladakh. It will observe the sky in both optical and infrared light.
